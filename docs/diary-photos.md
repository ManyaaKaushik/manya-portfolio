"About Me Diary" board in the NID SEM 4 Figma file (node 1781-7475).

The grandma page, Tiny Glade and Letterboxd reuse images already in `public/assets/`.

The Figma originals are small (most under 350px wide), so these will look soft on large
or high-density screens. Dropping higher-resolution versions in under the same names is
all it takes to sharpen them. The cover snapshots and the four "things I love" polaroids
exist only as combined strips in Figma; separate photos could become individual polaroids.

Page 4 of the board ("Photography wall — take content and pics from old portfolio") has
no photos in Figma, and manyaa.me doesn't carry any either, so it isn't in the diary yet.
The page after "What I can bring" is a plain "to be continued…" endpaper: page-flip needs
an even page count for the back cover to close the book, so remove it if you add a page.
